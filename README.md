# 👾 MONSTER QUEST

> A minimalist adventure where strategy meets survival. Clear the levels, avoid the beasts, and reach the goal.

---

## 🎮 Overview

**Monster Quest** is a level-based arcade game designed for quick sessions and challenging gameplay. Your objective is simple, but the monsters standing in your way are anything but.

### ✨ Features
* **Simple Levels:** Each level is easy for the player to solve
* **Simple Mechanics:** Easy to pick up, difficult to put down.
* **Clean Aesthetic:** A focused UI that keeps your eyes on the action.
* **Lightweight:** Runs smoothly on almost any hardware.

## 🎯 The Objective

Your mission is simple, but the path is dangerous. To conquer each level, you must master the following:

1.  **Search:** Explore the map to find the hidden keys.
2.  **Collect:** Pick up all **3 Keys** 🔑 scattered throughout the stage.
3.  **Escape:** Once you have all three, the **Exit Portal** will unlock. Reach it to advance to the next level!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Monsters are always watching. If you get caught before reaching the exit, you'll lose your keys and have to restart the level!
